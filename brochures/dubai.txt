OVERVIEW
Dubai, one of the seven emirates of the United Arab Emirates, is a city of
superlatives. From the world's tallest building to the largest shopping mall,
Dubai delivers extraordinary experiences with year-round sunshine, luxury
hospitality, and a perfect blend of Arabian heritage and futuristic architecture.
It is an ideal destination for luxury travellers, families, and adventure seekers.

TOP TOURIST ATTRACTIONS
- Burj Khalifa — world's tallest building (828m), observation decks on floors 124 and 148
- Dubai Mall — one of the world's largest shopping malls (with indoor ice rink and aquarium)
- Palm Jumeirah — iconic palm-shaped artificial island
- Dubai Frame — picture-frame shaped skyscraper with walk-on glass bridge
- Dubai Creek and Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood (old Dubai)
- Dubai Museum in Al Fahidi Fort
- Global Village (seasonal: October–April) — international pavilions and entertainment
- IMG Worlds of Adventure — world's largest indoor theme park
- Miracle Garden (seasonal) — world's largest flower garden
- The Dubai Fountain — world's largest choreographed fountain system

ACTIVITIES & EXPERIENCES
- Desert Safari with dune bashing, camel riding, and Bedouin camp dinner
- Hot air balloon ride over the Arabian desert at sunrise
- Ski Dubai — indoor ski resort inside the Mall of the Emirates
- Speedboat tour past iconic landmarks
- Dhow Cruise along Dubai Creek or the Marina
- Helicopter tour of the Palm Jumeirah and Burj Al Arab
- Deep-sea fishing excursion (Margie's exclusive charter)
- Skydiving over the Palm Jumeirah
- Gold and Spice Souk exploration in Deira

DINING HIGHLIGHTS
- Pierchic: Overwater seafood restaurant with Burj Al Arab backdrop
- At.mosphere: World's highest restaurant on Burj Khalifa floor 122
- Al Hadheerah: Traditional Emirati dinner in a desert fort setting
- La Petite Maison: European cuisine in DIFC financial district

BEST TIME TO VISIT
- November to March: Ideal weather (20-28°C), outdoor activities comfortable
- December to January: Dubai Shopping Festival, fireworks, street entertainment
- Avoid June to September: Extreme heat (40-48°C), high humidity

TRAVEL TIPS
- Dress modestly when visiting mosques and souks
- Alcohol is available in licensed hotels and restaurants only
- Tipping 10-15% is customary in restaurants
- Uber and Careem are widely used alongside taxis
- Currency is UAE Dirham (AED); most places accept major credit cards
