OVERVIEW
London, the capital of England and the United Kingdom, is one of the world's most
visited cities. Steeped in over 2,000 years of history, London blends iconic
landmarks with world-class culture, cuisine, and entertainment.

TOP TOURIST ATTRACTIONS
- Buckingham Palace and Changing of the Guard
- The Tower of London and Crown Jewels
- The British Museum (free entry)
- National Gallery, Trafalgar Square (free entry)
- Westminster Abbey and Houses of Parliament
- The Shard - panoramic city views
- Borough Market - London's oldest food market
- Notting Hill and Portobello Road Market
- Kensington Palace and Diana Memorial Garden
- Greenwich Observatory and the Prime Meridian

ACTIVITIES & EXPERIENCES
- West End Theatre Shows (Les Misérables, Hamilton, The Phantom of the Opera)
- Thames River Cruise from Westminster Pier
- Jack the Ripper walking tour in Whitechapel
- Harry Potter Studio Tour (Watford, 20 min from London)
- Afternoon Tea at Fortnum & Mason or The Ritz
- Cooking classes at the Jamie Oliver Cookery School
- Day trip to Stonehenge and Bath (Margie's exclusive coach tour)

BEST TIME TO VISIT
- June to August: Warm weather, long days, major events (Wimbledon, Trooping the Colour)
- September to November: Fewer crowds, mild temperatures, autumn colours
- December: Christmas markets, festive lights on Oxford Street

TRAVEL TIPS
- Use the Oyster card or contactless payment on all London transport
- Book The Shard and popular attractions in advance
- Many world-class museums are free of charge
- Tipping is appreciated but not mandatory (10-12% at restaurants)
