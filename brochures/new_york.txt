OVERVIEW
New York City, the city that never sleeps, is the most iconic urban destination
in the United States. From the glittering skyline of Manhattan to the diverse
neighborhoods of Brooklyn, NYC offers an unparalleled blend of culture,
entertainment, cuisine, and energy. It is home to world-famous landmarks,
Broadway theater, and some of the best restaurants on the planet.

TOP TOURIST ATTRACTIONS
- Statue of Liberty and Ellis Island (book ferry tickets in advance)
- Empire State Building Observatory
- Central Park (843 acres of urban parkland)
- The Metropolitan Museum of Art (The Met)
- Times Square and Broadway Theater District
- 9/11 Memorial and Museum
- Brooklyn Bridge and DUMBO neighborhood
- High Line Park - elevated rail park in Chelsea
- The Museum of Modern Art (MoMA)
- Rockefeller Center and Top of the Rock Observatory
- Coney Island (summer)
- One World Observatory (One World Trade Center)

ACTIVITIES & EXPERIENCES

- Cycling across the Brooklyn Bridge
- New York Yankees or Mets baseball game (seasonal)
- Comedy Club night in the West Village
- Day trip to the Hamptons (Margie's exclusive summer package)
- Guided Harlem Gospel Tour on Sundays
- Night photography tour of illuminated Manhattan

NEIGHBORHOODS TO EXPLORE
- Greenwich Village: Bohemian cafés, Washington Square Park
- Soho: Art galleries, designer boutiques, cast-iron architecture
- Williamsburg: Craft breweries, street art, rooftop bars
- Chinatown & Little Italy: Authentic dining, vibrant street markets
- Harlem: Jazz clubs, Apollo Theater, soul food

BEST TIME TO VISIT
- April to June: Spring flowers in Central Park, comfortable temperatures
- September to November: Ideal weather, leaf colors in Central Park
- December: Holiday window displays, Rockefeller Christmas tree, ice skating

TRAVEL TIPS
- Buy a 7-day MetroCard for unlimited subway and bus travel
- Many museums offer free evenings (MoMA on Fridays, The Met — pay what you wish)
- Yellow cabs and rideshares are plentiful, but the subway is fastest
- Reserve popular Broadway shows months in advance for best seats
