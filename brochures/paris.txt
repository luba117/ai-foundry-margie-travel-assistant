OVERVIEW
Paris, the City of Light, is synonymous with romance, art, and gastronomy.
France's capital enchants visitors with its elegant boulevards, world-famous
museums, iconic landmarks, and exceptional cuisine. Paris is consistently
ranked among the top travel destinations in the world.

TOP TOURIST ATTRACTIONS
- Eiffel Tower (book summit tickets in advance)
- The Louvre Museum - home to the Mona Lisa
- Notre-Dame Cathedral (under restoration, exterior viewing)
- Palace of Versailles (30 min by RER from Paris)
- Musée d'Orsay - world's finest Impressionist art collection
- Arc de Triomphe and the Champs-Élysées
- Sacré-Cœur Basilica, Montmartre
- Centre Pompidou - modern and contemporary art
- Sainte-Chapelle - stunning Gothic stained glass
- Père Lachaise Cemetery - final resting place of famous figures

DINING HIGHLIGHTS
- Café de Flore - legendary literary café in Saint-Germain-des-Prés
- L'As du Fallafel - best falafel in the Marais
- Breizh Café - award-winning crêperie
- Au Pied de Cochon - 24-hour Parisian brasserie near Les Halles

BEST TIME TO VISIT
- April to June: Spring blooms, pleasant weather, fewer crowds than summer
- September to October: Harvest season, fashion week, golden light
- December: Magical Christmas markets and illuminations

TRAVEL TIPS
- Validate your Metro ticket before boarding to avoid fines
- Many attractions require advance online booking
- Learn a few words of French — locals greatly appreciate the effort
- Avoid tourist traps around the Eiffel Tower for dining
