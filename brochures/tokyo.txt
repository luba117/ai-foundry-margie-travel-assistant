OVERVIEW
Tokyo, Japan's bustling capital, is one of the most fascinating cities on Earth.
A seamless blend of ultramodern and traditional, Tokyo dazzles visitors with its
neon-lit skyscrapers, serene temples, world-renowned cuisine, and impeccable
efficiency. It is consistently ranked as one of the world's safest and most
innovative cities.

TOP TOURIST ATTRACTIONS
- Senso-ji Temple, Asakusa — Tokyo's most famous Buddhist temple
- Shibuya Crossing — world's busiest pedestrian scramble crossing
- Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden
- Tokyo Skytree (634m — tallest structure in Japan)
- Meiji Shrine, Harajuku
- teamLab Planets or teamLab Borderless — immersive digital art
- Tsukiji Outer Market — fresh sushi and street food
- Ueno Park and Zoo (cherry blossom season highlight)
- Akihabara Electric Town
- Imperial Palace East Gardens

CUISINE HIGHLIGHTS
- Ramen: From rich tonkotsu to light shoyu broths
- Sushi and sashimi at Tsukiji or high-end Ginza restaurants
- Tempura: Light, crispy and expertly fried
- Yakitori: Grilled chicken skewers at izakayas
- Conveyor belt sushi (kaiten-zushi) for budget dining
- Wagyu beef at Asakusa specialty restaurants

BEST TIME TO VISIT
- March to May: Cherry blossom (sakura) season — magical but book early
- October to November: Crisp autumn colors, comfortable temperatures, fewer crowds
- December: Winter illuminations and festive atmosphere

TRAVEL TIPS
- Get a Suica or Pasmo IC card for seamless transport across metro, buses, and trains
- Japan Rail Pass recommended for day trips (Hakone, Nikko, Kyoto)
- Carry cash — many local shops and restaurants are cash-only
- Respect local customs: remove shoes when entering homes and many traditional venues
